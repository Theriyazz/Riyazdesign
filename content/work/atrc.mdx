<ContextCard>
  For ATRC, Abu Dhabi's research and development body, I designed a STEM career test for school students, solo, from working prototype to developer handoff.

  ATRC set the test at 25 questions. My job was to carry a student all the way through.
</ContextCard>

## 01 — The problem

- **Attention drifts, a notification lands, the tab closes, and twenty minutes of honest answers disappear.**
- **The brief was a contradiction: a fixed 25 questions, experienced as something a student wants to finish.**

## 02 — Prototype first

I built two working prototypes in Lovable, one consumer-toned and one institutional, because a test flow can't be judged from static screens.

### Three findings from clicking through them

<Findings>
  <Finding n="01" title="State the cost before asking for the effort">
    Telling students the time, question count and “no registration” before the start button made starting feel safe.
  </Finding>
  <Finding n="02" title="A selected answer should still look like an answer">
    One prototype flooded the chosen option and dropped its badge. The final keeps the badge and marks selection with fill and border.
  </Finding>
  <Finding n="03" title="A verdict closes doors; a ranking opens them">
    One prototype ended with one career, the other ranked every path. Ranked is more honest for a teenager, so it became the three-pathway report.
  </Finding>
</Findings>

## 05 — The final design

<Decision

### Four question types in rotation

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Standard choice">Five options with letter badges. The baseline format.</Card>

### What I learned

<Takeaways>
  <Takeaway title="Watch five students take it">
    Every finding came from testing my own prototypes. Next time I'd watch real students, and measure completion first.
  </Takeaway>
  <Takeaway title="Prototype the final interactions too">
    Drag-to-rank and build-a-sentence went to development from Figma.
  </Takeaway>
  <Takeaway title="Prototype-first is a research method">
    These findings are invisible in static screens. A rough build cost two days and bought the strategy.
  </Takeaway>
</Takeaways>
